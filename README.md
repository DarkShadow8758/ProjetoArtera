# 🎨 Artera

> An educational 3D adventure game that transforms art history into an interactive journey.

## 📖 About the Project

**Artera** is an educational adventure game developed in Unity for PC, where players explore different artistic movements through an immersive narrative experience.

The story follows a protagonist who awakens in a strange and fragmented world, uncertain of where she is or how she arrived there. Throughout her journey, she travels across environments inspired by historical artistic movements, beginning with **Cubism**.

Each environment features unique visual, architectural, and conceptual characteristics, allowing players to naturally learn about art while exploring the world.

One of the game's main mechanics is a **camera system**, used to capture important artworks and elements discovered during the adventure. Every photograph contributes to rebuilding an **interactive gallery**, gradually revealing information about each artistic movement and its historical significance.
